# Pomo Contributor License Agreement

Thanks for contributing to Pomo! This agreement explains what happens to the
code, translations, documentation or other material you send us. It is short on
purpose. Please read it before your first pull request.

## 1. Who is who

- **"You"** means the person (or the organization, if you contribute for your
  employer) sending a Contribution.
- **"Maintainer"** means Tunar Camalov, the author and owner of Pomo, and anyone
  Pomo is later transferred to (for example a company that takes over the
  project). This agreement passes to them automatically.
- **"Contribution"** means anything you submit to the Pomo repositories, through
  a pull request, an issue, a patch or any other way, that you mean to be
  included in the project.

## 2. You keep your copyright

You stay the owner of your Contribution. You can use it anywhere else, in any
way you like. This agreement gives the Maintainer a license; it does not take
your copyright away.

permanent, worldwide, non-exclusive, free and irrevocable license to use,
copy, modify, publish, distribute and sublicense your Contribution.

licenses other than the AGPL-3.0, including commercial or closed-source
licenses. The Maintainer needs this to run the hosted Pomo service, sell paid
plans and keep the project's options open in the future.

If your Contribution is covered by a patent you own or control, you give the
Maintainer and everyone who receives Pomo a permanent, worldwide, free and
irrevocable license to use that patent for Pomo. This license ends for anyone
who sues claiming that Pomo or your Contribution infringes a patent.

## 5. Our promise to you

Every version of Pomo that has been published under the AGPL-3.0 stays
available under the AGPL-3.0. A license change can only affect future
versions, never code that was already released.

## 6. What you confirm

By signing, you confirm that:

- the Contribution is your own original work, or you have the right to submit
  it under this agreement (for third-party code, say so clearly in the pull
  request, with its source and license);
- if your employer has rights to what you create, they have allowed you to
  make the Contribution, or they have signed this agreement too;
- you have the legal right to agree to this.

## 7. No obligations

The Maintainer does not have to use your Contribution. You give it "as is",
without warranties of any kind, and you do not have to support it.

## 8. How to sign

Tick the CLA box in the pull request template, or write this in your pull
request:

> I have read the Pomo CLA (CLA.md) and I agree to it.

Signing once covers all of your future Contributions. You do not have to sign
for issues that only report a bug or suggest an idea.
